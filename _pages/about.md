I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Sylhet, Bangladesh. I received my B.Sc. in Civil and Environmental Engineering from [Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST)](https://www.sust.edu/) in 2024 with a GPA of 3.76/4.00.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **soil stabilization**, **geohazard mitigation**, **soil remediation**, and **bio-geotechnics**. I am particularly interested in sustainable ground improvement techniques, including bio-cementation using cyanobacteria, chemical stabilization of problematic soils, and the use of waste materials in geotechnical applications.

## Current Research

At Leading University, I am currently involved in:

- **Experimental Characterization and Slope Hazard Assessment of Sylhet Region Using FEM** --- Identifying hazard-prone slope locations through geospatial and field reconnaissance, performing laboratory characterization of soil samples, and developing finite element models in PLAXIS 2D to assess failure probabilities and slope hazard mitigation strategies.

- **Effects of Sand Piles in Reclaimed Soil: A Case Analysis using FEM** --- Supervising and documenting an ongoing ground stabilization project, analyzing the effectiveness of sand piles, and building connections between observed, experimental, field, and simulation data.

## Recent Publications

My work has been published in journals including *Geotechnical and Geological Engineering* (Springer) and *Heliyon* (Elsevier), with additional manuscripts currently under review. My publications span topics such as desiccation cracking mitigation using fiber reinforcement, bio-cementation of sand via cyanobacteria-induced calcite precipitation, and waste glass stabilization for pavement geotechnics.
