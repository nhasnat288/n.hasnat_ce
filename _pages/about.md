I am **Nabid Hasnat** — a Civil and Environmental Engineering graduate from [Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST)](https://www.sust.edu/) and currently a **Lecturer** at the Department of Civil Engineering, [Leading University](https://www.lus.ac.bd/), Sylhet, Bangladesh.

My work focuses on sustainable ground improvement techniques, including bio-cementation using cyanobacteria, chemical stabilization of problematic soils, and the use of waste materials in geotechnical applications. I am passionate about bridging experimental geotechnics with computational methods.

I am open to research collaborations. Feel free to reach out!

## Research Interests

- Soil Stabilization
- Geohazard Mitigation
- Soil Remediation
- Bio-geotechnics
