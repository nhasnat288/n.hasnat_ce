    <p>This project investigates the feasibility of partially replacing natural sand with fine-grained crushed waste glass (CWG), both alone and in combination with fly ash (FA), for geotechnical applications in pavement subgrade. The research addresses both waste management and sustainable construction material challenges.</p>
    <p><strong>Key areas:</strong> Waste glass utilization, fly ash stabilization, sustainable geotechnical materials, pavement subgrade improvement</p>
